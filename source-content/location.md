# Location 

## Spooners range

 The only entrance to Kohatu Motorcycle Centre is to take the turnoff to Olivers Road from Motueka Valley Highway. Olivers road is approximately 1.1km from Kohatu Flat Rock Cafe and approx. 6.6 km from Tapawera. (Image above.) 

 On Olivers Road keep heading left and follow road to Kohatu Park. Go through Kohatu Park gate to Stanley Brook Road, keep heading north past the Nelson Car Club to Kohatu Motorcycle Centre.
