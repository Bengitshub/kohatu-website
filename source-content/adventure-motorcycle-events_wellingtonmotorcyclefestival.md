# Wellington motorcycle festival

 1-Day Ride [/adventure-motorcycle-events/category/1-Day+Ride] 

 7 Aug 

Written By HotHouse [/adventure-motorcycle-events?author=573a408b3c44d82cb7032a92]

#### Date: 30th and 31st May 2026

An event for all classes and types of motorcycles. 

Adventure biking courses over two days exploring council parks, forestry and private land not normally accessible to Adventure riders.

Poker run on Saturday starting in Trentham and Masterton and ending at Brewtown. Seven locations to get to in order to collect a playing card, discard two and present your best Poker hand to win prizes

On the evening of Saturday the 30th we will gather all riders to the Greenspace of Brewtown to enjoy static displays from clubs and vendors while the brews and food trucks supply food and drinks. 

Adventure bikes cost $350

Road bikes $45
