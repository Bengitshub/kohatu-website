# Sounds Like

 1-Day Ride [/adventure-motorcycle-events/category/1-Day+Ride] 

 7 Aug 

Written By HotHouse [/adventure-motorcycle-events?author=573a408b3c44d82cb7032a92]

#### Date: 21st and 22nd February 2026

This full 2 day ride will be on a mixture of , private farm tracks and gravel roads in and around the Marlborough’s Admiralty Bay and French Pass. The off road areas on based on 3 large outer sounds farms including Te Kuta and Port Ligar. Overnighting at the French Pass hall for whom we will be fundraising to support the local community.

The event fee includes the accommodation, breakfast and dinner on the night. GPX file to be provided and a luggage and support vehicle. You will need to bring a mattress and sleeping bag taken in the support vehicle.

GPX files will be provided.

More information to follow. Costs $295
