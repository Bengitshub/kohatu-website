# Rainey River road and lakes station

 1-Day Ride [/adventure-motorcycle-events/category/1-Day+Ride] 

 7 Aug 

Written By HotHouse [/adventure-motorcycle-events?author=573a408b3c44d82cb7032a92]

#### Date: 16th november 2025

This day will start with crossing a private farm, going over a high pylon track and exiting out another large farm. We will then make our way along the ridges of Lake Station and the neighboring property to exit near Lake Rotoiti. After lunch in St Arnaud it will be an afternoon run down the Rainbow road and up to the Rainbow ski field to hopefully see a lack of snow before returning to Nelson.

Details to follow along with GPX 

Fundraising for a local group via Lakes Station owners $130 total cost. $22.50 Extra if you want a run up to the Rainbow Ski field at the end of the day. (Goes to the club for road maintenance and a DOC fee.)
