# tempello

 1-Day Ride [/adventure-motorcycle-events/category/1-Day+Ride] 

 7 Aug 

Written By HotHouse [/adventure-motorcycle-events?author=573a408b3c44d82cb7032a92]

#### Date: 13th december 2025

We will leave Nelson and head over the Maungatapu. We ride all the way to Blenheim entering the Tempello Station near Fairhall, going over the station (Approx 30km’s) and exiting the station in the Awatere Valley before heading up Mt Altimarloc for Lunch. Returning the same way to Nelson

Lunch to be provided on Altimarloch as part of the cost. $130

Final scouting to be completed to provide GPX files.
