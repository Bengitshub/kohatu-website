## Looking for your next off-road fix?

KohatuMC events deliver unforgettable two-wheeled adventures across a variety of New Zealand’s best terrain. We specialise in experiences that combine challenge, scenery, and solid community vibes.

Whether you're chasing the thrill of a big climb, testing your skills on technical single track, or exploring backcountry routes on your ADV bike — our events are designed for riders who want more than just a race.

Join KohatuMC Events for adventure motorcycle and trail rides across Nelson and New Zealand. See upcoming events.

And we’re not stuck in one location — we go where the trails and terrain are best.
