# The Five Year Plan (6 Days of riding)

 6-Day Ride [/adventure-motorcycle-events/category/6-Day+Ride] 

 16 Jan 

Written By HotHouse [/adventure-motorcycle-events?author=573a408b3c44d82cb7032a92]

#### Date: 15th February 2026

Starting Year 1 at Cape Reinga, we’re setting out on a 5-year journey to ride the length of New Zealand. Each year, we’ll tackle a new section of the country, exploring off-road tracks, private land, and scenic public trails, with plenty of rest days in between to recharge.

Here’s the breakdown:

**Year 1 (2026)**: Cape Reinga - Northland

**Year 2 (2027)**: Central North Island

**Year 3 (2028)**: Lower North Island

**Year 4 (2029)**: Upper South Island

**Year 5 (2030)**: Lower South Island – **Prize giving on Stewart Island**

Each year, we’ll start from Cape Reinga to kick off the leg, but you can join for that year's section or challenge yourself by starting from the top and completing the entire journey. For example, in Year 5, you can choose to join us for 1 week in the Lower South Island, or start at Cape Reinga and enjoy 5 weeks of riding!

 **Final Year Prize Giving** will be held on Stewart Island — a celebration of every rider who completed the adventure!

**What’s Included:**

* 6 nights of accommodation

* Dinner on 6 evenings

* GPS Route Downloads for navigation

* Two sweep riders for safety

* All land access fees and permissions

* Luggage van to handle your gear

* A rest day between each week of riding to recharge!

This is more than a motorcycle ride; it’s an adventure, a challenge, and a chance to see New Zealand like never before.
