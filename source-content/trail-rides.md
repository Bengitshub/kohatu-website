# trail rides 

#### Round 1-Anything Mechanical 

 We’re heading to the hills for another great ride — this time near St Arnaud , at the top of the South Island. Join us on Saturday 6th December for a full day of off-road motorcycling through a mix of private farmland, forest, and back-country trails that are open for one day only . 

 This ride has been designed for trail and enduro riders who enjoy real trail terrain, with options to suit a range of skill levels. Expect forest tracks, open climbs, river crossings, and incredible views across the Nelson Lakes region . 

 Date: Sunday 7th December (New Date due to weather)
 Location: 2323 Korere Tophouse Road
 Sign-On: From 7:30am
 Riders’ Briefing: 8:15am
 Start: 8:30am
 Finish: 3:00pm

#### Trail Information 

- 
 Main Loop: Ideal for competent off-road riders 

- 
 Optional Sections: More technical tracks for those who want a challenge 

- 
 Support Crew: Marshals, recovery, and first aid on site 

#### What to Bring 

Off-road motorcycle in good condition

- 
Full riding gear (helmet, boots, gloves, body protection)

- 
Extra fuel, water, and basic tools

- 
A positive attitude and sense of adventure
