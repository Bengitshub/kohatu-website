A one day trip starting at Nelson Motorcycles in Nelson. Registration and briefing to be held at 8:00am with stands up at 8:30am. GPX files to be provided to follow the route below with meeting points at St Arnauds petrol Station, Lake Rotoroa, Mt Ella Station and Murchison Pub for lunch..

88 Valley Road to St Arnaud,

Porika to Rotoroa,

The Braeburn,

Tutaki Road to Mt Ella Station Private Farm,

Tractor (if you want to keep your feet dry)

South Matakitaki Valley Private Farm to Murchison Lunch,

Lake Matiri thru DOC and Power Station private land.

Sign out and make your way home from North Murchison.

237km's to sign out (allow for travel back to Nelson)

Cost $120,

Allow extra for lunch and tractor ride if required ($20)
