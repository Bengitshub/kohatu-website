# Anything Mechanical

 1-Day Ride [/adventure-motorcycle-events/category/1-Day+Ride] 

 7 Aug 

Written By HotHouse [/adventure-motorcycle-events?author=573a408b3c44d82cb7032a92]

#### Date: 18th october 2025

Event registration and briefing will be held at Nelson Motorcycles at 8:30am with stands up at 9:00am. GPX files will be issued prior to the event and we will provide sweep riders and medical support. 

Starting in Nelson, turn off at Brightwater, River terrace Road, Wairoa Gorge Road.
Pig Valley Road and into Matt Dicks farm. 5km's farm tracks with a tricky mud climb to deal with, good tyres required.
Descending down the other side on Ian Parkes Farm into 88 Valley Road.
Next is a climb up to the Inwoods Lookout at around 1,000m.
Then a short road ride all the way to the Anything Mechanical farm (Nicholls Family) for 3 hours of exploring. There is a 6km loop but 55km's of other tracks to explore.
We will then back track over the Parkes and Dicks farms.
Sign out will be at the Sprig and Fern in Brightwater.

Cost to be $110 unless you have ridden with us before then it will be $100 ($20 of which to the farm to help with track maintenance)

Please bring you own lunch and water for the 3 hours at Anything Mechanical.
