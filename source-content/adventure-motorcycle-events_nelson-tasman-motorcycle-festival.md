# Nelson Tasman Motorcycle Festival

 2-Day Ride [/adventure-motorcycle-events/category/2-Day+Ride] 

 7 Aug 

Written By HotHouse [/adventure-motorcycle-events?author=573a408b3c44d82cb7032a92]

#### Date: 9th and 10th May 2026

Including Road bikes (all classes) as well as the usual Adventure bikes for 2026. Road bikes to take part in a Treasue Hunt around the Nelson Tasman area on Saturday the 9th while the Adventure bikes will ride both Saturday and Sunday. All bikes to return and ride into Founders to celebrate all things to do with Motorcycles including, static displays, Vendors and food trucks.

Open to the public and raising funds for Nelson Tasman Search and rescue.

Please fill in the form below and nominate your preferred class of bike.

Adventure bike cost $350

Road Bike Cost $45
