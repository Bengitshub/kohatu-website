Adventure programme

Adventure bikes ride private-land courses on both days. Entry NZ$350 — suitable off-road-capable adventure bikes; grading and requirements confirmed with the entry pack.

Road-bike programme

Road bikes of every class join the Saturday treasure hunt around Nelson Tasman. Entry NZ$45 — open to all road-registered bikes.

The finish

Everyone rolls into Founders Heritage Park together — static displays, vendors and food trucks, open to the public. The festival raises funds for Nelson Tasman Search & Rescue.
