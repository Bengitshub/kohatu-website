The ride

Two full days on private farm tracks and gravel in and around Marlborough's Admiralty Bay and French Pass. The off-road runs across three large outer-Sounds farms including Te Kuta and Port Ligar — country you can't reach any other way, with the Sounds below you most of the day.

The overnight

We stay at the French Pass hall, and the event fundraises to support the community that hosts us — dinner and breakfast are included. Bring a mattress and sleeping bag; they travel in the support vehicle.

What to bring

  • Road-registered adventure bike with current registration and WoF
  • Full riding gear
  • Mattress and sleeping bag
  • GPS loaded with the supplied GPX route
