The series

Torque and Trails is Kohatu's e-mountain-bike uphill racing format, held at selected trail locations around Nelson. The first published event was Round One of a proposed three-round series at Codgers.

Previous format

  • E-mountain bikes only
  • Uphill racing on selected downhill trails
  • Four timed climbs and three descents in the first published round
  • An outright fastest-time competition
  • A separate handicap competition based on rider age, rider weight and bike torque
  • Sponsor-supported prizes and an evening prizegiving
