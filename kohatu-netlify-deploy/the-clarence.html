The ride

Three full days on a mixture of road, private farm tracks and gravel through country you simply cannot ride any other way — the Awatere, the Clarence Valley's remote stations, and the St James country into Hanmer. We're working with local community groups along the route, so your entry feeds directly back into the districts we ride through.

Day by day

Day 1 — Nelson to Kekerengu · ~200 km
Over the Maungatapu to Blenheim, then into Tempello Station to reach the Awatere Valley and cross the Awatere River (conditions allowing). South to Kekerengu, where the local community hosts us for the night — dinner and breakfast included, bunking down in the shearers' quarters or on mats in the hall.
Day 2 — The Clarence Valley to Hanmer · ~200 km
South through three private stations in the Clarence Valley. We cross the Clarence River — by tractor if the water's up — exit through Muzzle Station and Blind Saddle onto the Inland Kaikoura Road, then over another private farm across the St James Range into Hanmer Springs. Accommodation included.
Day 3 — Hanmer to Nelson · ~350 km
Out on the Lewis Pass road and into Glenhope Station for 55 km of backcountry tracks with a lunch stop provided. Exit at Boyle Village and cruise home via Maruia Saddle, Murchison, Braeburn and Porika.

Is this ride for me?

Grade 3 — Station rider. You should be comfortable standing on the pegs for long stretches, riding rutted farm climbs and crossing rivers. It's a big-distance ride, not a technical enduro — but weather can turn farm track into hard work fast. Good knobby-ish tyres are a must, and your bike needs a fuel range of 250 km+ between stops.

What to bring

  • A road-registered, warranted and insured adventure bike in good condition
  • Full riding gear — helmet, boots, gloves, body protection
  • Sleeping bag and pillowcase for the shearers' quarters night
  • Basic tools, spare tube/plugs, and water for full days out
  • Your GPS or phone mount loaded with the supplied GPX route

Your overnight bag travels in the luggage van — keep the bike light.

Good to know

What happens if weather closes a section?
Routes cross live rivers and working stations, so sections can change on the day. We scout ahead, and sweep riders carry comms — if a crossing isn't safe, there's always a plan B.
What's the refund policy?
A refund less a $40 admin fee is available for medical or similar reasons up to 14 days before the event. Inside 14 days, refunds only when a replacement rider is confirmed. Full details in the terms.
Do I need any special licence or paperwork?
A current motorcycle licence and a road-legal bike (rego + WOF) — much of the route links public roads between the private sections. You'll sign a land-access waiver at sign-on; it's part of what keeps the station gates open to us.
