Know before you go

The Kohatu grading system

Every ride carries a grade so you can pick terrain that matches your skills and your bike. Be honest with yourself — overestimating is how good days go bad.

1

Gravel cruiser Any road-legal bike · new to off-road

Formed gravel roads and easy station driveways. If you can ride confidently on loose metal, you're in.

2

Backroad explorer Adventure bikes · some off-road experience

Farm two-track, paddock riding and shallow water splashes. Standing on the pegs should feel natural.

3

Station rider Most Kohatu adventure rides live here

Full days over hill-country stations: rutted climbs, river crossings, mud when it rains and the odd tricky descent. Good tyres and a good attitude required.

4

Single-track hunter Trail & enduro bikes · fit and experienced

Technical single track, steep forest climbs and rock gardens. Optional loops on many rides let you earn your bragging rights.

5

The hard yards Expert only · by invitation on selected rides

Challenge sections that will test bike, body and sense of humour. Walk it first. Then decide.

Kohatu Park · Spooners Range

The adventure training ground

5.5 km of purpose-built adventure-bike single track loaded with training features and challenges, right beside the Nelson Car Club gravel track — plus a kids' area for young riders finding their feet. Built by riders, for riders, on 11 hectares of Kohatu Park.

Before you turn up

What do I need to do before arriving at the park?
Email dave@kohatumc.co.nz to let us know you're coming — we'll only be in touch if there's an unforeseen closure. Questions? Call Dave on 027 448 6688.
What do I need to bring?
A roadworthy, warranted and insured bike (kids' bikes excepted), cash for the gate fee, and your own food and water. There's no cellphone coverage, so download the directions before you leave.
How do I get in?
Turn onto Olivers Road from the Motueka Valley Highway — 1.1 km from the Kohatu Flat Rock Cafe, 6.6 km from Tapawera. Keep left to Kohatu Park, through the gate to Stanley Brook Road, then north past the Nelson Car Club. The gravel road is a strict 50 km/h zone. On arrival your bike gets checked and you'll sign a liability form — then ride as many laps as you like.
Which bikes does the park cater for?
Adventure and enduro bikes, with a children's fun area for kids' bikes and quads. Under-12s ride with an adult present.
Can I book the park for a private event or training day?
Yes — we're happy to open any day for groups of six or more. Email Dave and we'll do our best to accommodate your crew.

Who's behind it

Built by riders, run by riders

Kohatu Motorcycle Centre started the way most good ideas do — a rider (that's Dave) looking at the Top of the South's endless hill country and wondering why there was nowhere to train for it. With a lease on 11 hectares at Kohatu Park, a crew of volunteer riders and machinery lent by locals, the training ground opened in 2023.

The events grew from there: local landowners opened their gates, the rides got longer, and now the calendar runs from one-day blasts to a five-year ride down the length of the country. Every ride is scouted, supported and finished with a story worth telling.
