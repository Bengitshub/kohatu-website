For Dave

A working replacement for kohatumc.co.nz

The site around this page is a live preview built on spec by Webhero — this page is the commercial side.

What you're looking at

Everything on this preview — every page, photo, film and event — was rebuilt from your existing website and public material. Dates, prices and inclusions are your own published figures, marked provisional until you confirm them; anything proposed new (the grading system, the e-MTB page framing, event statuses) is flagged for your sign-off.

What's already built

  • Mobile-first design in your brand, with your photography and Blake's films
  • Every offering on its own page: adventure rides, festivals, trail rides, Torque and Trails, the park
  • One event system — every card, page and Google listing reads from a single event record, so a date changes once and updates everywhere
  • Honest event statuses (provisional / entries open / sold out / completed) with matching buttons
  • A past-rides archive so delivered events keep selling the next ones
  • Ride grading (1–5) so riders self-qualify instead of phoning
  • Redirects mapped from every current kohatumc.co.nz URL — old Facebook links keep working
  • Fast static hosting, Google event listings, privacy statement, accessibility basics

The offer

NZ$3,950 fixed price to finish and launch this build on kohatumc.co.nz, including:

  • Two reasonable rounds of revisions to content and imagery
  • Domain connection and deployment
  • Ride-list form and mailing-list connection
  • Online entries connected through an NZ-based ticketing provider (recommendation on our call)
  • Basic analytics and Google Search Console setup
  • Redirects from every existing URL
  • Thirty days of post-launch defect support

Ongoing: an optional NZ$129/month care plan — hosting oversight, event and date updates, status changes, and new event pages as your calendar moves.

What you'd confirm before launch

All event dates, prices, inclusions and capacities; Five Year Plan price and information pack; Deep in the Sounds details; Torque and Trails locations and format; park entry and motorcycle requirements; grading; image and film permissions; privacy wording; participant terms and legal review; booking provider; mailing provider.

Information carried across from the existing Kohatu website reflects previously published material and must not be treated as confirmation that it remains current.
